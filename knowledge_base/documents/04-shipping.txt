Acme Support Shipping and Delivery

All Acme Support products are online services. There is no physical product and
no physical shipment. Access is granted to your account immediately after a
successful payment.

ONBOARDING AND ACCESS

When you start a new subscription, your administrator receives an email with
a sign-in link. Access is usually available within 5 minutes of payment. The
activation email is sent only to the address that completed the purchase.

SERVICE AVAILABILITY

Acme Support targets 99.9 percent monthly availability for Acme Desk and Acme
Flow, and 99.99 percent for Acme Vault. Maintenance windows are announced at
least 48 hours in advance and are scheduled outside business hours.

DATA REGION

Acme Support stores customer data in the United States by default. Customers on
Acme Vault may select the European Union or Australia as their data region
before onboarding. The data region cannot be changed after the first production
workspace is created.

CHANGING DATA REGION

To move your workspace to another region, contact Acme Support. A regional
migration takes approximately 10 business days and requires a short service
pause. Only Acme Vault customers can change region.

DOWNLOADING YOUR DATA

Account administrators can export workspace data as JSON or CSV files from the
Export page. Exports are available for 7 days. Exports contain support
conversations, account settings, and audit records. Exported files are not
password protected by Acme Support, so store them securely.
