# Acme Support — Account Management

## Signing in

Sign in with your email address and password. Sessions expire after 120 minutes
of inactivity. Signing in on a new device signs you out of the previous
session, so only one session is active per account at a time.

## Password requirements

Passwords must be at least 10 characters and at most 128 characters. Common
passwords are rejected. Passwords are never emailed, displayed, or stored in
plain text. Use the Forgot password link to set a new one using a reset link
that expires after 30 minutes.

## Two factor authentication

Two factor authentication is available on every plan and is required for
Acme Vault. Enable it from Security settings. You can use an authenticator app
or a hardware security key. Acme Support support staff cannot view or disable
your two factor authentication.

## Adding and removing team members

Administrators can invite team members from the People page. An invitation is
valid for 7 days. Inviting a member does not increase your bill until they
accept and are counted as an active seat. Removing a member immediately frees
their seat.

## Roles

There are two roles. A member can read and reply to support conversations.
An administrator can additionally manage billing, members, security settings,
and integrations. Only administrators can delete a workspace.

## Transferring administrator rights

An administrator can transfer administrator rights to another member from
People settings. The transfer takes effect immediately. At least one
administrator must remain on the workspace at all times.

## Deactivating your own account

Administrators can request deletion of their entire workspace from Settings.
Deletion removes conversations, members, and audit records permanently within
30 days. A confirmation step is required. Individual members cannot delete
themselves; ask an administrator to remove them instead.

## Audit records

Every administrative action is recorded in an audit log, including sign-ins,
role changes, exports, and deletions. Administrators can view and export the
audit log. Audit records cannot be edited or deleted by administrators.
