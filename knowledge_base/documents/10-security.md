# Acme Support — Security and Compliance

## Certifications

Acme Support maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certification. Current
reports are available to customers under NDA from the Trust page.

## Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer, and at rest with AES-256.
Encryption keys are rotated annually. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id and
are never stored in a recoverable form.

## Data retention

Support conversations are retained for 24 months by default. Audit records are
retained for seven years to meet audit requirements. Deleted data is removed
from primary storage within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.

## Access control

Acme Support staff access customer data only through a time-limited,
logged support session, and only with the customer's consent. Every staff access
is recorded in an immutable audit trail.

## Incident response

Acme Support maintains a documented incident response plan with a named
incident commander. Suspected security incidents are reported to customers
within 72 hours of confirmation, as required by applicable contract terms.

## Penetration testing

An independent third party performs penetration testing annually. Findings are
triaged as critical, high, medium, or low. Critical and high findings are
remediated within 30 days of confirmation.

## Subprocessors

Acme Support publishes its current list of subprocessors on the Trust page.
Customers are notified 30 days before a new subprocessor is added, and may
object in writing within that period.
