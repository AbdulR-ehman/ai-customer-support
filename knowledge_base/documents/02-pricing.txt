Acme Support Pricing and Billing FAQ

HOW DOES ACME SUPPORT BILL?

Acme Support bills monthly in advance by default. Annual billing is available
and reduces the per-user monthly price. Invoices are issued on the same calendar
day each month and are payable within 14 days of the invoice date.

WHICH PAYMENT METHODS ARE ACCEPTED?

Acme Support accepts major credit cards, and bank transfer for annual
contracts. We do not accept cash, cheques, or cryptocurrency.

DO YOU CHARGE TAX?

Prices are shown excluding sales tax. Where applicable, tax is calculated from
the billing address on your account and added to the invoice. If your billing
address changes, the new tax rate applies from the next invoice.

CAN I GET A QUOTE BEFORE BUYING?

Yes. Contact Acme Support for a written quote for 20 seats or more, or for an
annual Acme Vault contract. Quotes are valid for 30 days.

DO YOU OFFER DISCOUNTS?

Acme Support offers a 20 percent discount on annual billing compared with
monthly billing. We also offer a 10 percent discount for registered
non-profits and educational organisations. Contact support to apply.

HOW DO I VIEW MY INVOICES?

Signed-in account administrators can download every invoice from the Billing
page. Invoices are retained for seven years.

WHAT HAPPENS IF A PAYMENT FAILS?

If a payment fails, Acme Support retries the charge up to three times over ten
days. You receive an email after each failed attempt. If payment still fails
after the retries, the subscription is paused and your data is retained for 60
days so you can update your payment method and restore access.

CAN I SWITCH TO ANNUAL BILLING?

Yes. Switching from monthly to annual billing takes effect immediately and
produces a credit for the unused portion of the current month. Switching from
annual to monthly billing takes effect at the end of the annual term.
