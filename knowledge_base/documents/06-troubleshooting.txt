Acme Support Troubleshooting Guide

CONVERSATIONS ARE NOT APPEARING

Clear your browser cache and sign out, then sign in again. If conversations are
still missing, confirm you are signed in to the correct workspace. A support
conversation is only visible to members of the workspace that owns it.

EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS ARE NOT ARRIVING

Check the spam folder first. Then confirm your notification preferences on the
Notifications page. Notification emails are sent from notifications@acmesupport.example.
Add that address to your allow list. Enterprise mail servers sometimes delay
delivery by up to 15 minutes.

ATTACHMENT UPLOAD FAILS

Acme Support limits a single attachment to 25 MB. Check the file size. If the
file is under the limit, try exporting it again: some image and video files
produced by other tools contain a corrupt header that prevents upload. PDF, PNG,
JPEG, and plain text files upload reliably.

AUTOMATIONS DO NOT RUN

Automations pause automatically when a plan limit is reached, when a required
field is empty, or when a destination integration is disconnected. Open the
automation and review the Run history panel for the exact reason it did not run.

A WORKFLOW RUNS BUT SENDS NOTHING

Check the step conditions. A condition that excludes the record is the most
common cause. Use Run history to compare the record against the conditions that
did not match.

ERROR: SESSION EXPIRED

Sessions expire after 120 minutes of inactivity. Sign in again. If your session
expires repeatedly on a healthy connection, clear cookies for the site and
disable aggressive browser privacy extensions for this domain.

THE DASHBOARD SHOWS WRONG TOTALS

Totals refresh every 15 minutes. Force a refresh with the reload button. If the
totals are still incorrect after a manual refresh, the cause is usually a
filtered view that was left active. Clear all filters and reload.
