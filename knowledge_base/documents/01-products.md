# Acme Support — Product Catalogue

Acme Support sells three fictional products. Every product is a subscription
service delivered online; nothing is shipped physically.

## Acme Desk

Acme Desk is the standard tier for small teams. It includes shared inboxes,
saved replies, automated triage, and 5 GB of attachment storage. Acme Desk
costs $12 per user per month when billed monthly, or $10 per user per month when
billed annually. Acme Desk supports up to 10 seats on the Standard plan.

## Acme Flow

Acme Flow is the automation tier. It includes workflow builders, conditional
routing, and webhook integrations. Acme Flow costs $29 per user per month when
billed monthly, or $24 per user per month when billed annually. Acme Flow
includes 100 GB of attachment storage and unlimited automation runs.

## Acme Vault

Acme Vault is the security tier. It includes single sign-on, audit log export,
data residency choices, and priority incident response. Acme Vault costs $49
per user per month when billed monthly. Acme Vault is billed annually only, at
$45 per user per month.

## Plan changes

You can upgrade a plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are
prorated for the remainder of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the
start of the next billing period, so you keep your current features until then.

## Seats and licences

A seat is one named user with a login. You may deactivate a seat at any time and
reassign the licence to another person. Adding seats is prorated immediately.
Removing seats produces a credit that appears on your next invoice.
