# Acme Support — Contact Details and Support Hours

## Support channels

Email: support@acmesupport.example
Phone: +1 555 010 0142 (fictional number, reserved for examples)
Help centre: https://help.acmesupport.example
Status page: https://status.acmesupport.example

## Support hours

Standard support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 US Eastern
Time, excluding public holidays. Weekend requests are queued and answered on
the next business day.

## Plan support levels

Acme Desk and Acme Flow include standard support with a first response target
of one business day. Acme Vault includes priority support with a first response
target of 4 hours during business hours and 24 hours outside them.

## Critical incident reporting

For a suspected security incident or a complete outage, email
security@acmesupport.example with the word URGENT in the subject line. Critical
incident reporting is monitored 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

## Before you contact support

Have your account email address ready, and note the approximate time the problem
started. Do not send passwords, API keys, or payment card details in a support
request. Acme Support will never ask for your password.
