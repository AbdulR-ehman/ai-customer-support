# Acme Support — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Acme Support?

Acme Support is a fictional customer support platform used as sample data for
this portfolio project. It offers team inboxes, automated triage, workflow
automation, and security features for support teams.

## How long is a conversation kept?

Support conversations are kept for 24 months by default, then automatically
deleted. Administrators can request a different retention period of between 3
and 36 months. Deleting a conversation removes it immediately and permanently.

## Can I import my existing help desk?

Yes. Acme Support imports conversations, contacts, and tags from CSV files, and
can migrate a full help desk through the assisted migration service. Contact
support for assisted migration.

## Is there an API?

Yes. Acme Flow and Acme Vault include the REST API and webhook integrations.
Acme Desk supports read-only API access. API keys are created in developer
settings and can be revoked at any time without affecting other keys.

## How do I secure my account?

Enable two factor authentication, keep sessions short, and give each person
their own account instead of sharing a login. Acme Support never asks you to
share a password, and support staff cannot see your password.

## What is not covered by support?

Support cannot process refunds directly, cannot change your plan price, and
cannot reset another person's two factor authentication. Those actions require
an account administrator or a billing specialist.

## Is my data used for training?

Acme Support does not use customer content to train third party machine
learning models. Aggregate, de-identified usage statistics are used to improve
the product. You can opt out of aggregate statistics in Privacy settings.

## How is Acme Support different from its competitors?

Acme Support bundles support inboxes, automation, and security compliance in
one product, and includes every feature in every paid plan rather than selling
security modules separately.
