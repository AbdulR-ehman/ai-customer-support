# Acme Support — Service Plans Comparison

This document compares the three Acme Support plans feature by feature.

## INCLUDED FEATURES

Shared inbox: included on Acme Desk, Acme Flow, and Acme Vault.
Saved replies: included on Acme Desk, Acme Flow, and Acme Vault.
Automated triage: included on Acme Desk, Acme Flow, and Acme Vault.
Workflow automation: not included on Acme Desk. Included on Acme Flow and
Acme Vault.
Webhook integrations: not included on Acme Desk. Included on Acme Flow and
Acme Vault.
Attachment storage: 5 GB on Acme Desk, 100 GB on Acme Flow, and 1 TB on
Acme Vault.
Single sign-on: only included on Acme Vault.
Data residency choice: only included on Acme Vault.
Audit log export: included on Acme Flow and Acme Vault.
Priority support: only included on Acme Vault.

## SUPPORT RESPONSE TARGETS

Acme Desk: one business day.
Acme Flow: one business day.
Acme Vault: 4 hours during business hours, 24 hours outside business hours.

## PRICE COMPARISON

Acme Desk is $12 per user per month monthly, or $10 annually.
Acme Flow is $29 per user per month monthly, or $24 annually.
Acme Vault is $49 per user per month monthly, or $45 annually.
Annual billing on Acme Vault is required.

## WHICH PLAN SHOULD I CHOOSE?

Choose Acme Desk if you need a shared inbox and triage only. Choose Acme Flow if
you need automation and integrations. Choose Acme Vault if you need single
sign-on, data residency, or priority support.

## CAN I MIX PLANS?

No. Every member of a workspace must be on the same plan. Split distinct teams
into separate workspaces if they need different plans.
