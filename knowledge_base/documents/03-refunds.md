# Acme Support — Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Refund window

Acme Support accepts refunds within 30 days of the initial purchase date. A
refund request submitted after 30 days is declined, except where a documented
service failure prevented you from using the product.

## How refunds are issued

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Depending on your bank,
the credit usually appears within 5 to 10 business days. Acme Support does
not issue cash refunds or refunds to a different card or account.

## The 30 day calculation

The 30 day window starts on the day of your first payment, not the day you
created the account. Renewals are not eligible for a refund under the 30 day
policy because the service has already been delivered for that period.

## Cancellation

You can cancel a subscription at any time from the Billing page. Cancellation
stops the next renewal charge immediately. Your plan remains active until the
end of the period you have already paid for.

## Service failure exceptions

If Acme Support experienced a documented service incident that materially
affected your account and you contacted support during the incident, support
may offer a pro-rated credit or a full refund for the affected period. Service
credits are discretionary and require a support request.

## Chargeback disputes

If you dispute a charge with your bank, contact Acme Support first. Repeated
chargebacks on a valid subscription may result in the account being suspended
while the dispute is investigated.

## Subscription renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each paid period. We email a
reminder 7 days before a renewal charge. Turning off auto-renew keeps the
service active until the period ends and prevents the next charge.
